How it works

Three steps, about ninety seconds, entirely in your browser.

01

Hold your hand to the camera

A guided overlay shows you exactly how to position your hand — no special lighting or hardware required.

02

Run a 10-second guided task

Rest tremor, postural tremor, finger-tapping, forearm rotation, or a spiral-drawing test — modeled on the standard clinical exam.

03

See your trend over time

Every session is saved on your device only, building a private, exportable history you can share with a clinician.

Five tasks, one motion vocabulary

Each modeled on a standard item from the clinical motor exam.

Rest Tremor

Hand relaxed in the lap — measures involuntary oscillation at rest.

Postural Tremor

Arm extended forward — isolates tremor that appears against gravity.

Finger Tap

Thumb-to-index tapping speed and amplitude decay over the recording.

Pronation-Supination

Rapid palm-up / palm-down rotation, measuring rhythm consistency.

Spiral Drawing

Trace a spiral — deviation from the ideal path is a classic fine-motor signal.
